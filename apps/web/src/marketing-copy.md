## FAQ COPY

### Q: Is this just another chatbot?
**A:** Sovereign is built for one specific purpose: understanding yourself, your relationships, and the systems around you. Every response is grounded in what you've shared about yourself. It's personal AI, not generic chat.

### Q: How is this different from ChatGPT?
**A:** ChatGPT starts fresh each time. Sovereign learns how you think, what matters to you, and uses that context to give you answers tailored to your specific situation. It's built for depth, not breadth.

### Q: Is my data private?
**A:** Completely. Your data stays encrypted on your device and Sovereign's servers. We never use your data to train models or share it with third parties. You control what Sovereign knows about you.

### Q: Can I use Sovereign for my family or team?
**A:** Yes. Sovereign+ includes the ability to bring another person in (with their permission) to understand shared dynamics, or to see whole systems like a team or family.

### Q: What if I don't like an answer?
**A:** You can reject it. Sovereign shows you the reasoning behind every answer so you can accept, correct, or disagree. Over time, Sovereign learns what actually fits you.

### Q: Do you sell my data?
**A:** No. We don't sell, share, or use your data for training. Your conversations stay yours. We make money through Sovereign+ subscriptions, not by exploiting user data.

### Q: Can I delete my account?
**A:** Yes. You can download all your data or delete your account entirely at any time. No lock-in. No surprise policies.

### Q: What's the Baseline?
**A:** Your Baseline is the private reference Sovereign uses to understand how you actually think, decide, and relate. You create it once (by sharing your birth details and a few reflection prompts), and then every answer Sovereign gives is grounded in your patterns, not generic wisdom.

### Q: Do I have to share everything?
**A:** No. Sovereign works best with context, but you decide what to share. You can ask about specific moments without building a full picture, or you can use your Baseline to go deeper.

### Q: How do I bring someone else into Sovereign?
**A:** If you upgrade to Sovereign+, you can invite another person to share their Baseline and explore your shared dynamics together. They create their own private Baseline, then you both decide what Sovereign may use from each profile.

## EXCLUSION LIST (BANNED TERMS)

❌ **Never use:**
- "Chat" → Use "conversation" or "dialogue"
- "Prompt" → Use "inquiry" or "question"
- "AI response" → Use "synthesis" or "answer"
- "Dashboard" → Use "workspace" or "profile"
- "Features" → Use "capacities" or specific nouns ("Understand your people")
- "Tool" → Use "faculty" or just name it ("Baseline")
- "Bot" → Use "Sovereign" (the AI is a singular entity)
- "Message" → Use "turn" or "inquiry"
- "Happy to help" → Never use cheerful, obsequious language
